# Property Theft Coverage

Document ID: POLICY-PROPERTY-THEFT-01

## 1. Coverage

Theft coverage pays for personal property stolen from the insured dwelling
or, at reduced limits, from a vehicle or while traveling ("off-premises"
theft, covered at 10% of the personal property limit). Coverage includes
damage to the dwelling caused by forced entry during a burglary (broken
door, window).

## 2. Exclusions

Theft coverage does NOT apply to:
- Theft by a resident of the household or a person regularly employed on
  the premises (e.g. a live-in caretaker), except as provided by law.
- Loss of cash, unless specifically scheduled, beyond $200.
- Jewelry, watches, and furs beyond $1,500 combined, unless separately
  scheduled on a Valuable Items Endorsement.
- Theft from a dwelling under construction or vacant for more than 60
  consecutive days.
- Business property beyond $2,500, unless a home-business endorsement is on
  file.

## 3. Deductible & Limits

- Standard deductible: $500 per incident.
- Personal property coverage limit: 50% of dwelling coverage limit, up to
  $250,000.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

A police report is REQUIRED for all theft claims and must be filed within
48 hours of discovery. An itemized list of stolen property with
approximate purchase dates and values must be submitted; claims lacking
this itemization are routed to manual review.
