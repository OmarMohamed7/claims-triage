# Auto Comprehensive Coverage

Document ID: POLICY-AUTO-COMPREHENSIVE-01

## 1. Coverage

Comprehensive coverage pays for damage to the policyholder's vehicle from
causes other than collision, including: fire, falling objects, hail,
flooding, windstorm, vandalism, and contact with an animal (e.g. hitting a
deer). It also covers glass damage (windshield chips/cracks) and, as
described in the Auto Theft Coverage document, theft and attempted theft.

## 2. Exclusions

Comprehensive coverage does NOT apply to:
- Mechanical or electrical breakdown unrelated to a covered peril.
- Damage from freezing, unless caused by a covered weather event.
- Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration (tire wear, rust, corrosion).
- Loss occurring while the vehicle is used for commercial purposes without
  a commercial-use rider.
- Confiscation or destruction by government or civil authority.

## 3. Deductible & Limits

- Standard deductible: $250 per incident (glass-only claims may qualify for
  a $0 deductible under the Glass Endorsement, if elected).
- Coverage limit: actual cash value of the vehicle at time of loss, up to
  $75,000.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

Claims must be reported within 30 days of discovering the damage. Weather
-related claims (hail, flooding, windstorm) should reference the
corresponding date and location of the weather event where possible, as
this may be cross-checked against public weather records during review.
