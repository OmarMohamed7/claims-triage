# Property Water Damage Coverage

Document ID: POLICY-PROPERTY-WATER-01

## 1. Coverage

Water damage coverage pays for sudden and accidental water discharge from
plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or household appliances (e.g. a burst
pipe or failed water heater), including the cost of tearing out and
replacing the portion of the dwelling necessary to access and repair the
source. Coverage extends to resulting damage to personal property at 50%
of the dwelling coverage limit.

## 2. Exclusions

Water damage coverage does NOT apply to:
- Flood, meaning surface water, overflow of a body of water, or
  storm-driven surge (this requires separate flood insurance, typically
  through NFIP).
- Gradual leakage occurring over a period of more than 14 days, or damage
  the policyholder knew about and failed to remediate.
- Sewer or drain backup, unless the Sewer Backup Endorsement is on file.
- Water damage resulting from the policyholder's failure to maintain
  reasonable heat in the dwelling during freezing conditions, if the
  dwelling was left unoccupied.
- Mold remediation beyond $5,000, unless directly and immediately resulting
  from an otherwise-covered water discharge.

## 3. Deductible & Limits

- Standard deductible: $1,000 per incident.
- Dwelling coverage limit: as stated on the policy declarations page, up to
  $500,000 under this policy tier.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

Claims must be reported within 5 days of discovering the damage. The
policyholder must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage (e.g.
shutting off the water supply) once the source is discovered; failure to
do so may reduce the payable amount. Photos of the source of the leak are
required.
