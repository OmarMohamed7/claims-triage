# Auto Theft Coverage

Document ID: POLICY-AUTO-THEFT-01

## 1. Coverage

Theft coverage (part of Comprehensive Coverage) pays for the loss of the
policyholder's vehicle due to theft, and for damage caused by attempted
theft (e.g. broken window, forced ignition damage, stripped catalytic
converter). Personal belongings left inside the vehicle are NOT covered
under this policy — see the policyholder's homeowners/renters policy for
personal property coverage.

If the vehicle is recovered within 30 days of the theft report, the claim
converts to a damage claim for repairs rather than a total-loss payout.

## 2. Exclusions

Theft coverage does NOT apply to:
- Theft by a resident of the policyholder's household or a person listed
  as an authorized driver on the policy.
- Loss where the keys were left in the ignition or the vehicle was left
  running and unattended in a non-secured location.
- Theft occurring more than 30 days after the policy lapsed for
  non-payment.
- Mysterious disappearance without evidence of forced entry or a police
  report.

## 3. Deductible & Limits

- Standard deductible: $500 per incident.
- Coverage limit: actual cash value of the vehicle at time of loss, up to
  $75,000. Aftermarket equipment (stereo systems, custom rims) is covered
  only up to $1,000 combined unless separately scheduled.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

A police report is REQUIRED for all theft claims and must be filed within
48 hours of discovery. Claims without an accompanying police report number
will not be processed and are routed to manual review. The claimed vehicle
value must be supported by registration and, where available, maintenance
records.
