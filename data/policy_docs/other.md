# Other / Miscellaneous Claims

Document ID: POLICY-OTHER-01

## 1. Coverage

This document covers claim types that do not fall cleanly under Auto
Collision, Auto Theft, Auto Comprehensive, Property Fire, Property Water,
Property Theft, or Liability coverage — for example, claims involving
multiple coverage types, claims describing an ambiguous or unusual loss,
or claims where the loss type stated by the claimant does not match the
policy's defined coverage categories.

Such claims are not automatically denied. They should be evaluated against
the closest matching coverage type(s) above, and where coverage is
genuinely unclear, routed to a human reviewer rather than auto-adjudicated.

## 2. Exclusions

- Losses with no identifiable covered peril (e.g. general deterioration,
  unexplained loss with no supporting detail).
- Claims for coverage types not written under this policy at all (e.g. a
  flood claim when only fire/water-discharge coverage, not NFIP flood
  insurance, is in force).

## 3. Deductible & Limits

Not applicable at the document level — refer to the deductible and limit of
whichever specific coverage type the claim is ultimately matched to.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

Claims that cannot be confidently matched to a single coverage type within
this document set should be flagged with `is_covered = None` (inconclusive)
by the Policy Retrieval Agent rather than guessed, and escalated for human
review under the "ambiguous policy coverage" reason.
