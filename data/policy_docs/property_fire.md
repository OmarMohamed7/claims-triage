# Property Fire Coverage

Document ID: POLICY-PROPERTY-FIRE-01

## 1. Coverage

Fire coverage pays for direct physical loss to the insured dwelling and
attached structures caused by fire or smoke, including firefighting
efforts (e.g. water damage from extinguishing the fire). Coverage extends
to detached structures (garages, sheds) at 10% of the dwelling coverage
limit, and to personal property inside the dwelling at 50% of the dwelling
coverage limit.

Additional Living Expenses (ALE) are covered up to 20% of the dwelling
limit if the dwelling is rendered uninhabitable by a covered fire.

## 2. Exclusions

Fire coverage does NOT apply to:
- Fire caused intentionally by the policyholder or at the policyholder's
  direction.
- Loss where the dwelling had been vacant for more than 60 consecutive
  days prior to the fire, unless a vacancy permit was on file.
- Damage from a fire that started on a neighboring property and is
  primarily the neighboring property owner's liability (subrogation
  applies).
- Loss to property specifically excluded under a scheduled personal
  property endorsement (fine art, jewelry above stated limits).

## 3. Deductible & Limits

- Standard deductible: 1% of dwelling coverage limit or $1,000, whichever
  is greater.
- Dwelling coverage limit: as stated on the policy declarations page, up to
  $500,000 under this policy tier.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

Claims must be reported within 5 days of the fire. A fire department
incident report is required for any claim exceeding $5,000. Claims lacking
a fire department report and exceeding this threshold are routed to
manual review before payout.
