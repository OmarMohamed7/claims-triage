# Liability Coverage

Document ID: POLICY-LIABILITY-01

## 1. Coverage

Liability coverage pays on behalf of the policyholder for bodily injury or
property damage to third parties for which the policyholder is legally
responsible, arising out of an auto accident or a covered premises
incident. This includes the cost of the third party's medical expenses,
property repair, and, where applicable, the policyholder's legal defense
costs, up to the policy limit.

## 2. Exclusions

Liability coverage does NOT apply to:
- Injury or damage the policyholder intended to cause.
- Liability arising from the policyholder's business or professional
  activities, unless a business liability rider is on file.
- Contractual liability assumed by the policyholder beyond what would exist
  at law in the absence of the contract.
- Liability to a co-insured under the same policy (e.g. one household
  member suing another).
- Damage to property owned by, rented to, or in the care of the
  policyholder.

## 3. Deductible & Limits

- No deductible applies to liability payouts to third parties.
- Per-occurrence limit: $100,000 bodily injury / $50,000 property damage
  under this policy tier, unless a higher-limit endorsement is on file.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

Claims must be reported as soon as reasonably possible, and always within
14 days of the policyholder becoming aware of a potential claim (including
being notified of a lawsuit or demand letter). Any correspondence from the
third party or their attorney must be forwarded immediately; the
policyholder should not admit fault or negotiate directly.
