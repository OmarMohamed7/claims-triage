# Auto Collision Coverage

Document ID: POLICY-AUTO-COLLISION-01

## 1. Coverage

Collision coverage pays for damage to the policyholder's own vehicle
resulting from a collision with another vehicle or object, or from the
vehicle overturning, regardless of fault. This includes single-vehicle
accidents (e.g. striking a guardrail, pothole, or parked car).

Covered losses are paid up to the vehicle's actual cash value (ACV) at the
time of loss, less the applicable deductible.

## 2. Exclusions

Collision coverage does NOT apply to:
- Damage from normal wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or road debris
  that does not involve a collision (see Comprehensive Coverage).
- Damage sustained while the vehicle was being used for commercial
  ride-sharing or delivery purposes, unless a commercial-use rider is on
  file.
- Damage sustained while the vehicle was being operated by a driver not
  listed on the policy, unless that driver had reasonable belief they were
  entitled to operate the vehicle.
- Racing, speed contests, or off-road use.
- Intentional damage caused by the policyholder.

## 3. Deductible & Limits

- Standard deductible: $500 per incident (policyholder may elect $250 or
  $1,000 at binding).
- Coverage limit: actual cash value of the vehicle at time of loss, up to
  $75,000.

## 4. Claim Filing Requirements

Claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident date. A police
report is required for any collision involving another vehicle, a
pedestrian, or damage exceeding $2,000. Claims reported more than 60 days
after the incident date are subject to additional review before payout.
